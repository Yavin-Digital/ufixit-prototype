ER 8300802 Thermal Fuse for Whirlpool Range

Part No. ER 8300802

Part Purpose: Acts as a safety device that cuts off power to the range or oven if it overheats, preventing potential damage or fire.

Part location on appliance: Typically located inside the control panel or near the oven’s heating elements.

Part failure symptoms:

  • Oven or range not turning on.
  • Oven or range shutting off unexpectedly.
  • No heat in the oven.

Part diagnosis: Test the thermal fuse with a multimeter for continuity. If there is no continuity, the fuse has blown and needs to be replaced. Inspect the surrounding area for any signs of overheating or damage that could have caused the fuse to blow. Alternatively, call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
