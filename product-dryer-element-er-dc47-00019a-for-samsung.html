Part No. ER DC47-00019A    Dryer Heating Element for Samsung Dryers

Part purpose:

The heating element turns electricity into heat.

Part location on dryer:

The heating element is located under the drum on the right side of the door.  The top and front must be removed to access the heating element.

Part failure symptoms:

When the element fails the dryer turns but does not heat.  It is essential to confirm the heating element is bad since other components such as thermodiscs & fuses can produce the same symptom.  Also, check for 220 volts at the back of the dryer.  The dryer will run but not heat when 110 volts are present instead of 220 volts.  Cycle the dryer circuit breakers off and back on if 220 volts is not present.

Part diagnosis:

If there is no continuity through the heating element, it is bad. Visually inspect element for the break. Bring your “heating element” in for free confirmation that it is good/bad or call the U-FIX-IT store for free assistance with diagnosis.
