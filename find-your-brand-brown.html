BACK Brown Appliance Parts & Repair Solutions | U-FIX-IT Appliance Parts Accessories Air Conditioner Parts Dishwasher Accessories Dishwasher Parts Dryer Parts Furnace Parts Microwave Parts Range & Oven Parts Refrigerator Parts Washer Parts Brown Appliances More than 80 years ago, Grover Cleveland Brown opened up Brown Stove Works Inc., with the goal of not only making the very best stoves available but also providing quality customer service. That 1935 beginning in Cleveland, Tennessee, made them one of 60 American companies to be in the business of manufacturing stoves, and the only one in the country that’s still constructs ranges and is privately held. Commitment to Product Excellence Brown Appliances has established a long-standing reputation for producing reliable and functional kitchen appliances, with a particular focus on their popular stove and range lines. Known for their simplicity, durability, and efficient performance, the company’s stoves and similar appliances have become a trusted choice for home and commercial kitchens. Brown’s commitment to straightforward design and quality craftsmanship has made it a preferred option for homeowners seeking dependable appliances without unnecessary complexity. Their kitchen appliances are especially appreciated for their user-friendly features, including easy-to-use controls and consistent heating. While Brown appliances are engineered to last many years, occasional wear and tear may necessitate replacement parts to keep them in top working condition. At U-FIX-IT Appliance Parts, we understand the importance of maintaining the performance of your Brown appliances, which is why we offer a wide selection of stove parts for Brown. See our extensive inventory, which includes a range of appliance parts for Brown products. This includes stove knobs to burner elements. Check out our inventory-compatible parts to help you restore your appliance’s functionality quickly and easily. Always Innovating The fact that Brown hasn’t attempted to stray from what made it such a successful company means that they’ve been able to focus on making sure that every detail is covered along the way. That laser-like focus has helped inspire concepts like cordless ignition, which eases the trouble of gas range lighting through the use of batteries. That isn’t the only recent innovation to make it into Brown equipment, with Coil Logic serving as an easy way to keep an eye on range temperatures on electric models. This asset can play a key role in drastically reducing the number of cooking fires that can quickly engulf a kitchen. That puts the health of a home’s occupants and their life in potential danger. In that same vein, the Brown HeatSense also addresses the danger of fires taking place during the process of cooking. The stove helps get a sense of impending danger by assessing the heat from utensils on it, which results in the burner being turned on and off depending on that temperature. From a more aesthetic perspective, the ranges can blend right in with a countertop through the use of slide-in trim for either electric or gas ranges. Companies that attempt to cover appliances with a wide brush might overlook a small nuance like that, something that sets Brown apart. The different available models include: Freestanding-Gas and Electric: Both of these come in four different sizes–20 inches, 24 inches, 30 inches, and 36 inches. With most of the models coming in one of three colors: white, black, and bisque. The gas ranges can ignite through either electric or a pilot light, with the electric version having an option of a sealed surface burner. For those electric models, customers can be guaranteed to have front controls along with one eight-inch surface element and a trio of six-inch elements. Each have one-piece drip pans that can be cleaned with ease. Built-In Wall Ovens: Three different models, two of which are gas, with all of them 24 inches. Each has a clock/timer with a window/light, with the gas stoves offering a lower broiler and the electric a broil element. Cooktops: The gas cooktops come from either 9-volt or electronic ignition and open burners, with chrome options also available. Both electronic options here offer the coil elements. Elderly Models: All nine models come in white and in sizes of either 20, 24 or 30 inches. All use either Coil Logic or coil elements, with each size offering two models with storage and one without. Replacement Stove Parts for Brown Products and Appliances Keeping your stove and other kitchen appliances in top condition extends their lifespan and ensures consistent optimal performance. U-FIX-IT Appliance Parts offers a comprehensive selection of appliance parts for Brown kitchen products. We make it easy to find the parts you need to address any repair or replacement requirements. Here is a partial list of appliance parts for Brown stoves and other appliances: Oven Igniters: These stove works parts for Brown ranges and ovens are essential for starting the heating process. A functioning igniter provides reliable ignition, allowing the oven to reach and maintain the desired temperature quickly. Faulty igniters can lead to delayed or no heating, so replacing them ensures efficient and consistent oven performance. Control Knobs: Control knobs are the interface between you and your stove’s heat settings. Over time, they may become worn, cracked, or loose, making it challenging to set accurate temperatures. Replacing broken or worn control knobs helps you regain precise control over your cooking, ensuring each dish is cooked to perfection. Drip Pans: Positioned beneath the burners, drip pans are stove works parts for Brown that catch spills and splatters, protecting various surfaces. In addition, they make cleaning more accessible and help maintain the appearance of your stove by preventing stains and corrosion. Damaged or heavily soiled drip pans should be replaced to keep your stove looking and functioning like new. Bake/Broil Elements: These heating elements evenly distribute heat throughout the oven, whether baking or broiling. If your oven struggles to maintain consistent temperatures or has cold spots, a replacement bake or broil element may be needed to ensure even cooking. Burner Grates: Burner grates provide a stable platform for your cookware, supporting pans, and pots directly over the flame or heating element. Over time, grates can become warped or corroded from constant heat exposure. Replacing damaged grates helps maintain a stable cooking surface and ensures heat is distributed evenly to your cookware. Thermostats: Thermostats are essential appliance parts for Brown stoves that monitor and regulate oven temperature, allowing it to reach and hold the temperature you set. A malfunctioning thermostat can lead to temperature fluctuations, which may result in undercooked or overcooked food. Replacing it helps ensure that your oven stays at the precise temperature needed for perfect results. Pilot Assemblies: The pilot assembly is responsible for igniting the burners for older Brown stove models. A faulty pilot assembly may lead to difficulty lighting burners or issues with flame consistency. Replacing this part restores proper ignition and flame stability, making your stove safer and more reliable. Temperature Sensors: These components detect the internal temperature within your oven, relaying that information to the thermostat to maintain accurate heat levels. If your oven temperature seems inconsistent or takes too long to reach the desired level, replacing the temperature sensor can help achieve more consistent cooking performance. Door Gaskets: The door gasket seals the oven door, preventing heat from escaping. A worn or damaged gasket can lead to heat loss, making it harder for your oven to reach the desired temperature. Replacing the gasket ensures energy efficiency, faster preheating times, and even cooking. Switches and Relays: These components control various functions within your stove, such as powering heating elements and regulating current flow. If switches or relays malfunction, parts of your stove may not work as expected. Replacing faulty switches and relays helps ensure that each part of your stove operates as it should.
