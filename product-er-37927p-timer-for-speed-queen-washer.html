Part No. ER 37927P

Part Purpose: Controls the timing of the wash cycles, including fill, agitate, rinse, and spin functions.

Part location on appliance: Located inside the control panel of the washing machine.

Part failure symptoms: Washer not advancing through cycles, washer stuck on one cycle, or not starting.

Part diagnosis: Inspect the timer for visible damage or wear, listen for the timer motor running, and test the timer contacts with a multimeter for continuity. Alternatively, call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
