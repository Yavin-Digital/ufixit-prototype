What Is AEO — and Why It Matters Right Now

💬 Sales Talking Point

"When your customers ask Google, ChatGPT, or Siri a question, an AI reads hundreds of websites and picks one to quote. Right now, that quote goes to your competitor — not you. This report shows you exactly where you're invisible to AI, and what we've built to fix it."

Traditional SEO earns a blue-link ranking on page one of Google. AEO earns the zero-click citation — the sentence an AI answer engine quotes directly in its response, with the business name attached. As of 2026, Google AI Overviews appear on the majority of informational searches. ChatGPT and Perplexity are now primary research tools for millions of consumers. Businesses that are not structured for AI engines are invisible to this audience.

AEO and SEO reinforce each other. The writing structure that makes a page easy for an AI to quote also makes it easy for a human to skim and for Google to understand. Every fact, every Q&A, and every schema block in this report works double duty — improving traditional rankings and earning AI citations simultaneously.

The Three Things AI Engines Need to Trust a Business

  1. Entity clarity — the business must be described in explicit, unambiguous facts (name, location, services, credentials).
  2. Answer-ready content — pages must contain direct answers to the exact questions customers ask, not marketing copy.
  3. Structured data (schema) — JSON-LD markup tells AI engines what the business is, what it does, and who to credit.
