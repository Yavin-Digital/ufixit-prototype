ER 154576501 Lower Seal for Frigidaire/Electrolux Dishwasher

Part No. ER 154576501

Part Purpose: Prevents water from leaking out of the bottom of the dishwasher door during operation.

Part location on appliance: Located at the bottom of the dishwasher door, sealing the gap between the door and the dishwasher tub.

Part failure symptoms: Water leaking from the bottom of the dishwasher door, visible wear or damage to the seal.

Part diagnosis: Inspect the lower seal for cracks, tears, or deformation. Ensure it fits snugly against the door and tub. Alternatively, call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
