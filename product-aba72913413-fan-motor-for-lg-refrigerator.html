ABA72913413 Fan Motor for LG Refrigerator

Part No. ABA72913413

  • Purpose: This ice room fan motor assembly is designed for LG refrigerators to circulate cooling air through the ice room, maintaining optimal temperatures for ice production and storage.
  • Location: Located within the ice compartment of the refrigerator, mounted on a bracket assembly.
  • Failure Symptoms:
    • Ice melting or clumping together
    • Reduced ice production
    • Unusual noises emanating from the ice compartment
  • Diagnosis: Inspect the fan motor for signs of wear or obstruction. Ensure the fan blades move freely and are not impeded by ice buildup. Use a multimeter to test for electrical continuity; a lack of continuity indicates a faulty motor. Alternatively, contact the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
  • Cross Reference Info: Replaces part numbers AP5669958, 2692189, ABA72913408, ABA72913412, PS7321745
