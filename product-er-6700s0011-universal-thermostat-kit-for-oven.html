ER 6700S0011 Universal Thermostat Kit for Oven

Part No. ER 6700S0011

Part Purpose: This universal oven thermostat kit with a knob controls and regulates the oven’s temperature by turning the heating element on and off to maintain the set temperature.

Part location on appliance: Located inside the oven control panel, connected to the oven’s heating elements.

Part failure symptoms:

  • Oven not reaching the desired temperature.
  • Oven overheating or not heating at all.
  • Inconsistent cooking temperatures.

Part diagnosis: Inspect the thermostat for visible damage or wear, test it with a multimeter for continuity, and ensure it is calibrated correctly. If the thermostat is faulty, it should be replaced. Alternatively, call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
