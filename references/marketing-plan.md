## 3. Audience priorities

Serve audiences distinctly (inferred from live offers/CTAs — not invented personas). Priority order for prototype rooms below.

### Audience 1 — DIY homeowners (conversion-critical — parts + diagnosis)

**Job:** Diagnose washer/dryer/fridge/range/dishwasher/AC/furnace symptom; get the right part; prefer repair over replace.  
**Live location:** Home, appliance hubs, brand pages, Repair Tips / DIY, store landings.  
**What they need:** Free diagnosis promise in full; free part testing; model-number help; clear Ask An Expert + call/visit CTAs; honest “not field service” framing.  
**Primary path:** Home → Appliance hub / Brand → Ask An Expert or Call store → Buy part (shop hub / sample PDP pattern).  
**Do not:** invent success rates, stock %, or “#1 DIY” claims.

### Audience 2 — Walk-in / phone callers needing diagnosis before purchase

**Job:** Talk to a human who listens to symptoms and confirms the part.  
**Live location:** Home “We’re Here to Help,” Contact, 4 store pages, Team.  
**What they need:** Correct phones; hours including Sat exceptions; team faces/bios; testimonials (Erick, Kirby, Lisa, Rosamari — USE verbatim from CONTENT).  
**Primary path:** Contact / Location → Call / Visit → Free testing.  
**Do not:** invent wait times or “always in stock” beyond ORG CLAIM stocking language.

### Audience 3 — Online buyers (continental US shipping)

**Job:** Find part by model/brand; get price + shipping; order or request quote.  
**Live location:** Shop, product_cat hubs, Shipping, Shipping inquiry, sample PDPs.  
**What they need:** Same-day ship cutoff facts; continental US; shipping help phone **972.780.9096**; quote form; warranty/returns clarity.  
**Primary path:** Brand/Appliance hub → Shop/category → Shipping / quote → purchase pattern.  
**Do not:** proto 438 SKUs; invent shipping times beyond Shipping page.

### Audience 4 — Spanish-speaking customers (South Dallas emphasis)

**Job:** Get help in Spanish where available (Tony M. bilingual — South Dallas).  
**Live location:** Team bios, South Dallas store.  
**What they need:** Visible bilingual cue on South Dallas / Team; do not invent bilingual coverage at all four stores.  
**Primary path:** South Dallas / Team → Call **972.780.9096**.  
**Do not:** invent Spanish site version without client scope.

### Audience 5 — Trade / technicians (volume UNKNOWN)

**Job:** Source parts quickly; possibly commercial-use awareness (warranty shortens to 30 days).  
**Live location:** Brand hubs, HVAC brand names on brand hub, Warranty commercial clause.  
**What they need:** Honest commercial warranty disclosure; fast path to call/counter.  
**Primary path:** Brand/Category → Call / Visit.  
**Do not:** invent B2B program, trade pricing, or Marcone-style pro portal.

### Audience 6 — Reviewers / service-recovery customers

**Job:** Leave positive review or report a bad experience.  
**Live location:** `/share-feedback/` cluster.  
**What they need:** Clear funnel (add review vs bad-experience form).  
**Primary path:** Feedback hub → appropriate child.
