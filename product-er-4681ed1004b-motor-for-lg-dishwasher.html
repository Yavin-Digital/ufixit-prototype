ER 4681ED1004B Motor for LG Dishwasher

Part No. ER 4681ED1004B

Part Purpose: Powers the circulation pump that moves water through the spray arms to clean dishes in the dishwasher.

Part location on appliance: Located at the bottom of the dishwasher, typically near the center, connected to the circulation pump.

Part failure symptoms: Dishwasher not cleaning dishes properly, no water circulation, unusual noises from the motor area, or the dishwasher not starting.

Part diagnosis: Inspect the motor for visible damage or debris, listen for any unusual noises during operation, test the motor with a multimeter for continuity, or call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
