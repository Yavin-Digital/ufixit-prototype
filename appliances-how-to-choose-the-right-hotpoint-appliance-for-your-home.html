How to Maintain Your Hotpoint Laundry Appliances To extend the lifespan of your appliances and prevent common performance issues, keep these troubleshooting and maintenance tips in mind: Vibration and Noise : Usually caused by an unbalanced load. Heating Problems : If your dryer isn’t heating, it may be a faulty heating element or a tripped thermal fuse. For these internal hardware issues, sourcing a genuine replacement part is necessary. What Common Issues Affect Hotpoint Washer Dryers and How to Address Them? Common issues with Hotpoint washer dryers include: Unbalanced Loads : This can cause excessive noise and vibration.
