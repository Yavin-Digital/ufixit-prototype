Part details

CMP 8201756 Door Chute Kit for Refrigerator

Part No. CMP 8201756

Part Purpose: The door chute kit helps direct ice from the ice maker into the dispenser chute for easy dispensing through the refrigerator door.

Part location on appliance: Located in the freezer door, behind the ice dispenser mechanism.

Part failure symptoms:

  • Ice not dispensing properly.
  • Ice getting stuck in the chute.
  • Ice chute door not closing or sealing properly, leading to frost buildup.

Part diagnosis: Inspect the chute and related components for damage, misalignment, or blockages. Ensure the chute door closes and seals correctly. If damaged or malfunctioning, it should be replaced. Alternatively, call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
