A00044305 Drain Pump for Dishwasher

Part No. A00044305

  • Purpose: This drain pump is designed for dishwashers to remove water from the tub during the drain phase of the washing cycle.
  • Location: Located at the bottom of the dishwasher, connected to the drain hose and sump assembly.
  • Failure Symptoms:
    • Dishwasher not draining properly
    • Standing water at the bottom after a cycle
    • Unusual noises during the drain cycle
  • Diagnosis: Inspect the pump for blockages, damage, or debris. Test for electrical continuity using a multimeter; a lack of continuity indicates a faulty pump. Alternatively, contact the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
  • Cross Reference Info: Replaces part number AP5805766
