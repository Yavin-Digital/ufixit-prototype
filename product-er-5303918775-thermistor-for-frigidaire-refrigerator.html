ER 5303918775 Thermistor for Frigidaire Refrigerator

Part No. ER 5303918775

Part Purpose: Monitors the temperature inside the refrigerator and sends the information to the control board to regulate the cooling cycle.

Part location on appliance: Typically located inside the refrigerator or freezer compartment, near the evaporator coils or along the interior walls.

Part failure symptoms: Refrigerator or freezer not maintaining the correct temperature, fluctuating temperatures, or error codes related to the thermistor.

Part diagnosis: Test the thermistor with a multimeter to check for proper resistance at different temperatures. Inspect for visible damage or disconnections. Alternatively, call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
