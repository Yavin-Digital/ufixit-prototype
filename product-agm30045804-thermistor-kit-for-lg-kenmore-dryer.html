AGM30045804 Thermistor Kit for LG Kenmore Dryer

Part No. AGM30045804

  • Purpose: This high-limit thermostat and thermistor kit is designed for LG and Kenmore dryers. The high-limit thermostat monitors the dryer’s internal temperature, shutting off the heat source if temperatures become dangerously high to prevent overheating. The thermistor is a temperature sensor that helps regulate the dryer’s temperature during operation.
  • Location: Both components are typically located within the dryer’s heating element housing or blower housing, depending on the specific model.
  • Failure Symptoms:
    • Dryer not heating properly or not heating at all
    • Dryer shutting off during cycles
    • Excessive heat emanating from the dryer
  • Diagnosis: Inspect both the thermostat and thermistor for signs of damage or wear. Use a multimeter to test for continuity; a lack of continuity indicates a faulty component. Alternatively, contact the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
  • Cross Reference Info: Replaces part numbers 6323EL2001B, 6931EL3002B, 6931EL3003C, 6931EL3003F, 6931EL3004B
