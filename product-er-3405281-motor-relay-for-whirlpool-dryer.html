ER 3405281 Motor Relay for Whirlpool Dryer

Part No. ER 3405281

Part Purpose: Controls the operation of the dryer motor, allowing it to start and stop during the drying cycle.

Part location on appliance: Located inside the dryer control panel or near the motor.

Part failure symptoms: Dryer not starting, dryer motor not running, or dryer shutting off unexpectedly.

Part diagnosis: Inspect the motor relay for visible damage or burn marks. Test the relay with a multimeter for continuity and proper function. Alternatively, call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
