Part details

ER 611475 Rack Roller for Bosch Dishwasher

Part No. ER 611475

Part Purpose: Supports the dishwasher rack, allowing it to slide in and out smoothly.

Part location on appliance: Located on the sides or bottom of the dishwasher rack, attached to the rack itself or the dishwasher frame.

Part failure symptoms:

  • Dishwasher rack not sliding smoothly.
  • Rack falling off the tracks.
  • Visible wear or damage to the rollers.

Part diagnosis: Inspect the roller for visible wear, cracks, or damage. Ensure it is securely attached and functioning properly. If the roller is damaged, it should be replaced. Alternatively, call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
