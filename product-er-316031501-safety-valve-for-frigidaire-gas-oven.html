ER 316031501 Safety Valve for Frigidaire Gas Oven

Part No. ER 316031501

Part Purpose: Controls the flow of gas to the oven burner, ensuring safe and efficient operation of the gas oven.

Part location on appliance: Located inside the oven, typically near the burner assembly.

Part failure symptoms: Oven not heating, uneven heating, or gas smell when the oven is off.

Part diagnosis: Inspect the gas valve for visible damage or blockages. Test the valve with a multimeter for continuity and proper function. Ensure that the gas supply is turned off before inspecting. Alternatively, call the U-FIX-IT store for free diagnosis assistance.
