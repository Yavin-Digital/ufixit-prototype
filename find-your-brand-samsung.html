The company’s journey, however, began far from the world of appliances. Founded in 1938 as a small South Korean trucking company by Lee Byung-chul, Samsung quickly expanded its reach. Within just a decade, Lee diversified the business into textiles, food processing, retail, insurance, and securities, paving the way for Samsung’s growth into the global powerhouse we know today. Samsung entered the electronics field in the mid-1970s when it recognized the enormous television market in the United States. That introduction into American households was followed by a full line of home appliances in the late 1980s.
