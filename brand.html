Section 05

Voice & Tone

Neighborly, expert, straight. U-FIX-IT talks like the person at the parts counter who helps you save money: plain words, real part numbers, no hype.

  • Lead with help: “Not sure it’s the right part? Call us, diagnosis is free.”
  • Use the house line: “We have it, or we’ll get it!”
  • Say what is true: same-day shipping by 3 pm CST on in-stock parts; 1-year replacement warranty unless stated on the receipt.
  • Never invent discounts, ratings or countdowns.

Section 06

Usage Rules

Do

  • Full-color logo on white or a white plate
  • White knockout on Navy or Ink
  • Navy for structure, red for the buy action
  • Oswald for headings, real product photos on white

Don’t

  • Put the full-color logo straight on navy or red
  • Stretch, recolor or upscale the 228px raster
  • Paint large areas red
  • Use invented reviews, prices or claims
